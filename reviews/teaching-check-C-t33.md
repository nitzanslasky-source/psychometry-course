## Added (3)

1. **solve-q-r26-t33-01: the chord–distance–radius right triangle.** This comes back in t33-01, in the ring lesson (more-tools) and in tangent questions. Four sentences before the goodbye:
   - when a chord and its distance from the centre are given, draw the distance and the radius to the chord's end;
   - half the chord, the distance and the radius make a right triangle, with the radius as hypotenuse;
   - any two of them give the third.
   One board line, shown at its clause.
2. **solve-geo33-g078: two tangents from P make angle P plus the central angle DOE equal to 180°.** The reason is the two right angles in O D P E. Second case: 40° → 140°. One board row, `∠P + ∠DOE = 180°`.
3. **solve-geo33-g090: the converse of Pythagoras.** When all three sides are given, check Pythagoras first. The right angle is across from the longest side, so here that side is the diameter. Second case: 5, 12, 13 (25 + 144 = 169 = 13²). The sentence was split for W19.
