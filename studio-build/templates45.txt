=== vb-inf_v2_0284 [source: Autumn 2024 §1 Q7] answer=2
STEM: "Harel Tel Aviv," the strongest basketball club in the league, has for several seasons been signing the most promising young players from the smaller clubs, although it fields hardly any of them; the club does not need them for its own team, but its rivals, deprived of their best prospects, grow weaker every year. Which of the following business practices most resembles the club's tactic?
  (1) A chain of pharmacies buys up small independent pharmacies in every town it enters, so as to remove them from the market
  (2) A jewelry house buys the entire output of a diamond mine, far more than it can set, so that its competitors' jewelry has to be made from lesser stones
  (3) A car manufacturer buys its electronic components from a rival manufacturer, so that the rival will depend on the income and refrain from competing
  (4) A paint manufacturer buys a controlling share in the company that supplies its pigments, so as to lower the price it pays for them
EXPL: The club acquires a resource it does not use in order to deny it to competitors; only (2) buys what it cannot use for the sake of what rivals then lack. (1) removes competitors rather than their resources; (3) makes the rival dependent rather than deprived; (4) is about the buyer's own costs.

=== vb-inf_v2_0285 [source: Winter 2022 §2 Q8] answer=2
STEM: Yaron and Lihi visited a nature reserve laid out around a group of springs, and noticed that several rare species of dragonfly, found nowhere else in the region, breed there. Yaron: "I suppose the reserve is what produced them — the protection it gives has let species thrive here that could not survive outside." Lihi: "Your supposition is like supposing that joining a basketball team makes people tall." Which of the following claims best expresses what Lihi meant?
  (1) Rare species are rare everywhere, and there is no reason to look for a special explanation of their presence at the springs
  (2) The reserve was laid out around the springs precisely because the rare dragonflies were already breeding there
  (3) The dragonflies are rare because they are found only where springs of this kind exist, and such springs are few
  (4) The reserve protects the dragonflies from collectors, and it is collectors who made them rare elsewhere
EXPL: Tall people join basketball teams; the team does not make them tall — the causation runs from the trait to the membership.
