=== vb-inf_v2_0237 [source: Spring 2026 §1 Q17] answer=3
STEM: Under the by-laws of a certain town, the council meets once a week, on Monday, and any councillor may ask that the council debate a particular matter. Such requests must reach the chairman of the council through the council clerk, who is required to pass them to the chairman on the day they are lodged. A matter whose request has reached the chairman by noon on Thursday will be put down for debate on the Monday of the following week; if, however, the chairman determines that the matter requires more preparation than that allows — and only in such a case — he may defer the debate to the Monday after, but no later. Four cases of requests to debate a matter are set out below. In which of them were the by-laws certainly violated?
  (1) A councillor lodged her request with the clerk on Friday; the matter was not debated on the following Monday nor on the Monday after, although the clerk passed the request to the chairman on the day it was lodged
  (2) The matter was not debated on the Monday following the week of the request, although the chairman determined that it needed no extra preparation and although the request had been lodged with the clerk on Thursday
  (3) A councillor lodged his request with the clerk on Tuesday, and the matter was debated only on the Monday two and a half weeks later, the chairman having determined that it required more preparation
  (4) The matter was debated eight days after the request was passed to the chairman, although he had not determined that any circumstance justified deferring it
EXPL: A Tuesday request reaches the chairman on Tuesday, so the debate is due the following Monday or, with a determination, the Monday after — never two and a half weeks later. (1) a Friday request misses the Thursday deadline, so the by-laws promise nothing for the two Mondays named; (2) "lodged on Thursday" does not establish that it reached the chairman by noon; (4) a request passed on, say, Friday carries no obligation for the coming Monday, so a debate eight days later may be the first Monday it was due.
