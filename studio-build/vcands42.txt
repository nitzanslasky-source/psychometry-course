=== vb-inf_v2_0406 [real exam | set inf_v2 | Claims and Logic] answer=2
STEM: A pair of claims is called "complete" if the first can be inferred from the second and the second from the first. Which of the following pairs of claims is complete?
  (1) A. There is something that is not in my possession. B. There is nothing that is in my possession.
  (2) A. If it rains, Liron is happy. B. If Liron is not happy, it is not raining.
  (3) A. Everything that is not red is tasty. B. Everything that is tasty is not red.
  (4) A. There are no professors who are not absent-minded. B. There are absent-minded professors.
EXPL: A conditional and its contrapositive are equivalent. (3) pairs a conditional with its converse; (4) runs one way only; (1) are different claims.

  (2) Are there students who were born in the summer and do not like playing football?

=== vb-inf_v2_0410 [real exam | set inf_v2 | Claims and Logic] answer=3
STEM: Three colors — yellow, blue and red — are primary colors, and three colors — green, purple and orange — are not. In a shop Dina found four tins of paint in four different colors out of the six named above, one color in each tin. What is certainly true of the colors Dina found?
  (1) At most one of them is not a primary color
  (2) Two of them are primary colors and two are not
  (3) At least one of them is not a primary color
  (4) Exactly one of them is a primary color
EXPL: Four different colors cannot all come from a set of three; so at least one is non-primary — and by the same argument at least one is primary. (1), (2) and (4) each fix a count the data leave open.
