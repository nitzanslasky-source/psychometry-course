## 2. New teaching
**What a fraction means** (video `fraction-basics`)
- New slide **Splitting the top**: (6+5)/7 = 6/7 + 5/7; (12+8)/4 = 3+2 = 5; never split the bottom: 7/(3+4) ≠ 7/3 + 7/4.
- New slide **Negative fractions**: −3/4 = (−3)/4 = 3/(−4); two minus signs cancel; a minus in front belongs to the whole top.
- Recap rebuilt with these two rules. New card **Fraction basics**, placed after the video. Before this, the first card came in lesson 3.
- Guided **Question 1** (q-r26-t02-01, (−6+15)/(−3)) with its solution video. Practice: q-r26-t02-02 (which is NOT equal to −2/5) and q-r26-t02-03 ((48+36)/12). Exam level in the practice section: q-r26-t02-04.

**Multiplying and dividing** (video `fraction-multiply`)
- New slide **"Of" means times**: 2/3 of 36 = 24, and "spend 1/3, then 1/4 of the rest": 3/4 · 2/3 = 1/2 left. It also shows the trap 1 − 1/3 − 1/4.
- Recap now includes "Of → times; of the rest → of what is left". New card **Multiplying & dividing**.
- Guided **Question 2** (q-r26-t02-05, water tank, "of the remaining") with its solution video. Practice: -06 (3/4 of 48), -07 (2/5 of a number is 24), -09 (workers). Exam level: -08 (book, "of the remaining pages", work backward) and -24 (girls/boys with glasses).

**Adding and subtracting** (video `fraction-add`)
- Slide 5 now names the **LCM** ("least common multiple = the smallest common denominator"). The written solutions use this word.
- New slide **Fraction bar = brackets**: (1 + 1/2)/(2 − 1/4) = 6/7.
- New slide **Mixed operations**: 3/4 − 1/2 · 2/3 = 5/12, and the wrong order (1/6).
- The recap now gives ÷ for division and adds "× ÷ first; a fraction bar is brackets". The card **Fraction rules** gets rows for "of" and several operations, plus LCM and shortcut tips.
- Guided **Question 3** (q-r26-t02-10, stacked fraction with sums) with its solution video. Practice: -14, -11. Exam level: -12 (5/6 − 2/3 ÷ 4/5 = 0) and -13 (the "continued" fraction 1/(1+1/(1+1/2))).
