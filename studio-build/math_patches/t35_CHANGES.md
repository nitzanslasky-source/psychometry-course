# Topic 35 — Three-dimensional geometry: changes (course review 2026-09)

## 1. Wrong rule fixed
- **Q9 video, slide 5 "Three angles in a cube"**: the board said "Edge and a diagonal of *another* face → 90°". That is false in general (edge AB and top diagonal EG make 45°). New wording everywhere (board, speech, memory card, new lesson): "An edge + a diagonal of the face it stands on, from the same corner → 90°". Added a spoken warning: they must meet at one corner, and the edge must stand on that face. The 60° line now says "two face diagonals from the same corner".
- **Q10 video, slide 2**: "an edge and a diagonal of another face" → "edge AE stands on the top face, and EG is a diagonal of that face, from the same corner E".

## 2. New methods (3 new lessons, 3 guided questions with solution videos)
- **New lesson "Water Level"** (after Question 1): water height = volume ÷ base area, pouring (the volume stays, not the height), dropping in a solid (rise = object volume ÷ base area), units (1 ml = 1 cm³, 1 liter = 1000 cm³, 1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³).
  Guided question: water poured from a 10×8 box (9 cm high) into a cube with edge 12 → 5 cm.
- **New lesson "Cones: Slant Height and Turning"** (after the pyramid-height question): r² + h² = ℓ² (the slant height is not the height); a rectangle turned about a side → cylinder; a right triangle turned about a leg → cone (the side on the axis is the height, and which leg you use matters).
  Guided question: a cone with r = 6 and slant height 10 → 96π.
- **New lesson "Cube and Box Facts"** (after the corner-cube question, before the memory card): a√2, a√3, box diagonal √(a²+b²+c²); the three cube angles (fixed wording); faces/edges/vertices of prisms (n+2, 3n, 2n) and pyramids (n+1, 2n, n+1); the painted cube (8, 12(n−2), 6(n−2)², (n−2)³); three face areas → volume = √(product); shortest path on the surface (unfold the faces, cube: a√5); quick elimination checks (height < slant edge, part < whole, bricks: try each position, same units first).
  Guided question: painted 5×5×5 cube, exactly one painted face → 54.
- **Lesson "Solids: Surface and Volume"**: new slide "Lying down" (same prism turned over: bases = the two identical faces, height = the distance between them); 1 ml = 1 cm³ added to the Volume slide; new slide "On the formula page" (what the official NITE page prints: box/cube volume and surface area, cylinder lateral/total/volume, cone and pyramid volume; what it does not: general prism rules, diagonals, cube angles, units); one line added to the recap.
- **"Solids in Questions"**: the "What's ahead" list now mentions water level, units, slant height and cube facts.
- Q8 video: board line + spoken reminder "Silver (45°-45°-90°): hypotenuse = leg × √2".
- Q10 video slide 3: explains why 18/√3 = 18√3/3 = 6√3, and the same with √2 (18/√2 = 9√2).
- Q10 video slide 4: one-step box diagonal √(6²+8²+24²) = 26 added; "5, 12, 13" / "3, 4, 5" written on the board next to the Hebrew mnemonic.
- Q4 video: spoken lines — the prism lies on its side; the height is edge BE = 4.
