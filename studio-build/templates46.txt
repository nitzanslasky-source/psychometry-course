=== vb-inf_v2_0366 [source: Spring 2026 §2 Q7] answer=1
STEM: In a study, Dr. Mor divided solvers of crossword puzzles into two groups — one of four members and one of ten — and asked each group to solve, working together, a crossword of the same kind. She found that the group of ten completed far more of the clues in the time allowed than the group of four, and she put forward the hypothesis that the size of a group influences its success at such tasks. Which of the following claims strengthens Dr. Mor's hypothesis?
  (1) The members of the group of four were experienced solvers, whereas the members of the group of ten had rarely attempted a crossword before
  (2) Six of the members of the group of ten left the room before the group had begun to work on the crossword
  (3) One of the members of the group of ten had seen the crossword before the study and knew several of the answers in advance
  (4) The crossword given to the group of four was noticeably harder than the crossword given to the group of ten
EXPL: If the larger group won despite being the less experienced, the size of the group is a stronger candidate for the cause. (3), (2) and (4) each supply a rival explanation of the result — prior knowledge, an effective group of four, an easier puzzle.

=== vb-inf_v2_0367 [source: Winter 2025 §2 Q11] answer=1
STEM: In country A an experiment was conducted with infants between one and two years old, in which it was found that many of them recognize themselves in a mirror: the researchers painted a small mark on each infant's cheek without his noticing, and after a while set him before a mirror. Most of the infants reached for the mark on their own cheek, and did not try to wipe it from the mirror. When the researchers repeated the experiment in country B, however, most of the infants neither touched the mark nor tried to remove it. The researchers concluded that in country B the ability to recognize oneself develops later than in country A. Which of the following findings does NOT weaken the researchers' conclusion?
  (1) In country A the proportion of infants who suffer from impaired vision is much higher than in country B
  (2) In country B it is customary to decorate infants' faces with painted marks, whereas in country A it is not
  (3) In another experiment, infants from country B succeeded in recognizing themselves in photographs, whereas infants from country A did not
  (4) Unlike infants in country A, infants in country B tend not to act without an explicit sign of permission
EXPL: Poorer vision in A would make A's success harder to obtain, not easier — it leaves the conclusion intact or strengthens it. (3) shows the ability present in B; (2) and (4) explain B's behavior without any difference in recognition.
