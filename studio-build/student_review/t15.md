### Q 1 (q-423): In Maya's class, \(\frac{1}{3}\) of the students are girls who play chess, and among those, \(\frac{1}{4}\) also play the piano. What could be the number of students in the class?
  (1) \(45\)
  (2) \(40\)
  (3) \(48\)
  (4) \(26\)
  correct: 2
  solution: The total must be divisible by 3 (so that a third of it is an integer of students), and that third must itself be divisible by 4. Together: the total must be divisible by 3·4 = 12 . Check: 48 = 12·4 ✓ ( 48:3 = 16 , and 16:4 = 4 ). The others fail: 45 is divisible by 3 but 15 isn't divisible by 4; 40 and 26 aren't divisible by 3 at all.

### Q 3 (q-425): a and b are positive integers. x is the remainder of a divided by 7, and y is the remainder of b divided by 4. What is the maximal value of the expression \(x \cdot  y\)?
  (1) \(6\)
  (2) \(18\)
  (3) \(3\)
  (4) \(28\)
  correct: 1
  solution: The largest possible remainder when dividing by 7 is 6 (one less than the divisor), and the largest when dividing by 4 is 3. Both maxima are achievable (e.g., a = 6 , b = 3 ), so the maximal product is 6·3 = 18 .

### Q 4 (q-426): A number leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 3. Which of the following expressions could represent the number (x is an integer)?
  (1) \(3\,x + 5\)
  (2) \(6\,x + 8\)
  (3) \({\left(3\,x + 4\right)}^{2}\)
  (4) \({\left(3\,x + 6\right)}^{2}\)
  correct: 2
  solution: Reduce each expression modulo 3. (1) 3x + 5 = 3(x + 1) + 2 — remainder 2, never 1. (2) 6x + 8 = 3(2x + 2) + 2 — remainder 2, never 1. (3) (3x + 4)² = 9x² + 24x + 16 = 3(3x² + 8x + 5) + 1 — remainder 1, always ✓. (4) (3x + 6)² = 9(x + 2)² — remainder 0, never 1.

  correct: 2
  solution: Write n = 4k : n(n + 4) = 4k(4k + 4) = 16·k(k + 1) . The numbers k and k + 1 are consecutive, so their product is always even — contributing one more factor of 2. Hence 32 divides n(n + 4) always. Tightness: n = 4 (k = 1) gives 4·8 = 32 exactly, so 64 is not guaranteed.

### Q None (q-450): A mother has x candies. She tries to divide them equally among her 3 children, but 1 candy remains left over.
