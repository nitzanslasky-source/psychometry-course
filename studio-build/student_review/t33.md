### Q 1 (geo33-g076): A, B and C lie on a circle with center O, with B on the minor arc AC. Given: angle AOC is 112°. What is angle ABC?
  [FIGURE: fig/geo33-g076.png - An inscribed angle intercepts the major arc]
  (1) \(68°\)
  (2) \(112°\)
  (3) \(124°\)
  (4) \(56°\)
  correct: 2
  solution: ABC intercepts the arc AC not containing B. Its central measure is 360°−112°=248°. The inscribed angle is half of 248°, or 124°.

### Q 3 (geo33-g080): The numerical value of a circle’s area in cm² is 3 times as much as the numerical value of its circumference in cm. What is its radius (in cm)?
