### Q 1 (geo35-g121): A cone-shaped container with base radius \(\sqrt7\) cm and height 9 cm is full of water. Which of the following empty containers can hold all the water? The dimensions given are internal dimensions.
  [FIGURE: fig/geo35-g121.png - Right circular cone]
  (1) A rectangular box measuring 7 cm × 3 cm × 3 cm
  (2) A square pyramid with base edge 6 cm and height 6 cm
  (3) A cube with edge 4 cm
  (4) A cylinder with base radius \(\sqrt5\) cm and height 4 cm
  correct: 1
  solution: The cone holds \(\frac{\pi(\sqrt7)^2\times9}{3}=21\pi\) cm³. The four candidate capacities are \(64\), \(20\pi\), \(63\), and \(72\) cm³. Since \(65.1<21\pi<66\), only the square pyramid has enough capacity.

### Q 2 (geo35-g122): A cone and a cylinder have equal base areas. The volume of the cone is twice the volume of the cylinder. If the cylinder’s height is h, what is the cone’s height?
  [FIGURE: fig/geo35-g122.png - A cone and cylinder with equal circular bases]
  (1) \(\frac{2h}{3}\)
  (2) \(6h\)
  (3) \(2h\)
  (4) \(3h\)
  correct: 1
  solution: Let the common base area be B and the cone height be H. Then \(\frac{BH}{3}=2Bh\). Divide by the positive B and multiply by 3 to obtain \(H=6h\).

### Q 3 (geo35-g123): The volume of rectangular box ABCDEFGH is 216 cm³. M lies on AB, AM : MB = 1 : 2, and N lies directly above M on EF. What is the volume of triangular prism AMD–ENH (in cm³)?
  [FIGURE: fig/geo35-g123.png - Rectangular box with hidden edges shown dashed]
  (1) \(72\)
  (2) \(108\)
  (3) \(36\)
  (4) \(54\)
  correct: 2
  solution: AM is one third of AB. Triangle AMD has half the area of the rectangle with sides AM and AD, so it has one sixth of the box’s base area. The prism and box have the same height. Its volume is therefore 216 : 6 = 36 cm³.

### Q 4 (geo35-g124): The bases of a right triangular prism are right triangles. Each base has a leg of 5 cm and a hypotenuse of 13 cm. The height of the prism is 4 cm. What is its lateral surface area (in cm²)?
  [FIGURE: fig/geo35-g124.png - A right triangular prism with congruent triangular bases]
  (1) \(120\)
  (2) \(60\)
  (3) \(144\)
  (4) \(180\)
  correct: 0
  solution: The missing base leg is 12, since 5² + 12² = 13². The base perimeter is 5 + 12 + 13 = 30. The lateral surface area is 30 × 4 = 120 cm².
