### Q 1 (q-248): \(\frac{{9}^{6} \cdot  {8}^{-2}}{{3}^{8} \cdot  {4}^{-2}} = ?\)
  (1) \({3}^{4} \cdot  {2}^{-2}\)
  (2) \({3}^{-2} \cdot  {2}^{4}\)
  (3) \(\frac{{2}^{2}}{{3}^{4}}\)
  (4) \(\frac{{3}^{4}}{{2}^{5}}\)

  solution: Convert every base to a prime: 9⁶ = (3²)⁶ = 3¹² , 8⁻² = (2³)⁻² = 2⁻⁶ , 4⁻² = (2²)⁻² = 2⁻⁴ . The fraction becomes (3¹² · 2⁻⁶) : (3⁸ · 2⁻⁴) = 3^(12−8) · 2^(−6−(−4)) = 3⁴ · 2⁻² .

### Q 3 (q-250): Given: \({4}^{2\,x} = {\left(\frac{1}{8}\right)}^{4 - 2\,x}\). \(x = ?\)

### Q 5 (q-252): Given: \(x \ne  0\) and \(x\sqrt{5} = 5\,\sqrt{x}\). \(x = ?\)
