### Q 1 (wp28-g124): How many three-digit numbers can be made from the digits 2, 4, 6 and 8 if the hundreds digit is smaller than the tens digit and the tens digit is smaller than the units digit?
  (1) 4
  (2) 6
  (3) 12
  (4) 24
  correct: 0
  solution: If the first digit is two, the possibilities are 246, 248 and 268. If the first digit is four, the only possibility is 468. Starting with six or eight leaves too few larger digits to finish. There are four numbers. The strict increases also mean no digit can repeat.

### Q 2 (wp28-g125): A café offers 4 sandwich fillings and 6 drinks. A meal contains one filling and one drink. How many different meals are possible?
  (1) 10
  (2) 16
  (3) 36
  (4) 24
  correct: 3
  solution: Choose the filling first: four options. For each filling, all six drinks remain available. That gives six meals for the first filling, another six for the second, another six for the third, and another six for the fourth. Four times six is twenty-four.
  solution: Adding four and six would count individual menu items, not combinations of one of each. A complete meal requires both a filling choice and a drink choice, which is why the choices multiply.

### Q 3 (wp28-g126): A two-character access code uses decimal digits from 0 to 9. The sum of its two digits must be 12. How many codes are possible?
  (1) 12
  (2) 7
  (3) 6
  (4) 8
  correct: 1
  solution: The first digit can be three through nine. A smaller first digit would require a second digit above nine. Once the first digit is fixed, the second must be twelve minus it, so only one choice remains. Seven first-digit options times one completion gives seven codes.

### Q 6 (wp28-g130): In how many different ways can 6 distinct framed photographs be placed in a row?
  (1) 120
  (2) 360
  (3) 720
  (4) 36
  correct: 2
  solution: Choose any of the six photographs for the first position. Five remain for the second, then four, three, two and finally one. Their product is 720. The final factor of one matters conceptually even though it does not change the product: the last photograph is forced.
  solution: This descending product is called six factorial, written with an exclamation mark. It is a short name for a multiplication, not a new mysterious operation. Six factorial means six times five times four times three times two times one.

### Q 8 (wp28-g133): How many diagonals does a seven-sided polygon have?
  (1) 14
  (2) 7
  (3) 21
  (4) 28
  correct: 0
  solution: From one vertex, a diagonal cannot end at that same vertex or either of its two neighbours. Four of the other six vertices remain available. Seven vertices each give four diagonal endpoints, but every diagonal is counted from both ends. Divide 7×4 by two to get fourteen.

### Q 16 (wp28-g143): In a chess event, each pair of players plays exactly one game. There are 45 games altogether. How many players take part?

  (2) 10
  (3) 8
  (4) 9
  correct: 1

  solution: We need two consecutive positive integers whose product is ninety. Nine times ten is ninety, so n is ten, the larger of the consecutive pair.
