### Q 1 (geo37-g156): The vertices of triangle ABC are A(4, 7), B(−8, −5), and C(13, −5). What is its perimeter?
  [FIGURE: fig/geo37-g156.png - Coordinate plane]
  (1) \(36+12\sqrt2\)
  (2) \(21+12\sqrt2\)
  (3) \(36+6\sqrt2\)
  (4) \(48\)
  correct: 0
  solution: BC = 13 − (−8) = 21. The altitude from A to the horizontal base has length 7 − (−5) = 12 and meets it at H(4,−5). BH = 12, so AB = 12√2. HC = 9, so AC = 15 by the 9–12–15 triple. Sum: 36 + 12√2.

### Q 2 (geo37-g158): A circle of radius 10 is centered at the origin O. A lies on the circle in the first quadrant and has y-coordinate 5. What is the angle between OA and the positive x-axis?
  [FIGURE: fig/geo37-g158.png - Coordinate plane]

  (2) \(30°\)
  (3) \(45°\)
  (4) \(60°\)

  SAYS: The slope of a straight line on the coordinate plane is always constant.
  SAYS: If I cut it into equal pieces, the distance on the x-axis and the distance on the y-axis between every two neighbouring points — always the same.

  SAYS: I can split this line into stairs. Here's one step, a second, a third.
  SAYS: The stairs build triangles. And all these triangles are congruent — the hypotenuses are equal, and it's the same slope.
  SAYS: Identical steps. So the width of every step is the same, and the height of every step is the same.

  [on screen] Blank
