### Q 1 (wp23-g054): In a survey, 55% of people do not own a bicycle. Of the bicycle owners, 40% own an electric bicycle and the rest own only ordinary bicycles. What percentage of all surveyed people own only ordinary bicycles?
  (1) 18%
  (2) 40%
  (3) 60%
  (4) 27%
  correct: 3
  solution: Use one hundred people to represent the whole survey. Fifty-five do not own a bicycle, leaving forty-five owners. Forty percent of those forty-five have an electric bicycle: ten percent is four and a half, so forty percent is eighteen.
  solution: Ordinary-only owners are the remaining forty-five minus eighteen, or twenty-seven. Since our original sample represents one hundred, twenty-seven people correspond to 27% of the full survey. The forty percent in the question belongs to the smaller forty-five-person group, not to the original hundred.

### Q 4 (wp23-g059): A membership fee rises from 60 credits to 66, then to 72. How do the two percentage increases compare?
  (1) The first percentage increase is larger
  (2) The increases are equal
  (3) The second percentage increase is larger
  (4) Their sizes cannot be compared
  correct: 0
  solution: Each increase adds six credits. The first rate is \(\frac6{60}=\frac1{10}=10\%\). The second is \(\frac6{66}=\frac1{11}\), which is smaller because the same positive numerator is divided by a larger denominator.
  solution: We do not need a decimal for one eleventh. The money increase stays the same, but its percentage shrinks when measured against the larger starting fee.

### Q 5 (wp23-g060): A player gives away 25% of her points. She then receives a bonus equal to 30% of the points she has left. Compared with her original score, what is her final score?
  (1) 5% higher
  (2) 2.5% higher
  (3) 7.5% lower
  (4) 2.5% lower
  correct: 3
  solution: Represent the original score by one hundred. Giving away twenty-five percent leaves seventy-five. The bonus is thirty percent of those seventy-five points: ten percent is seven and a half, so the bonus is twenty-two and a half.
  solution: The final score is seventy-five plus twenty-two and a half, or 97.5. That is 2.5 below the original hundred, so it is a 2.5% decrease. The later thirty-percent bonus uses the reduced score, which is why subtracting twenty-five from thirty gives the wrong result.

### Q 6 (wp23-g061): Rae earns 20% more than Sol. Tia earns \(16\frac23\)% less than Rae. What is Tia’s wage divided by Sol’s wage?
