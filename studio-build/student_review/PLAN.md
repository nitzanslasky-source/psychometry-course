# Math review — combined plan (topics 1–38)

Detailed reports: `review_t*.md` in this folder (each starts with "Depends on / Needed by").
Headline: **every answer key in all 38 topics is correct.** Weak and medium students are mostly well served; strong
students are not yet exam-ready because many exam-hard traps are never taught. The problems below are what to fix.

## Priority 2 — methods used in questions but never taught (add a slide or short video + practice)
- **Algebra:** quadratic equations and trinomial factoring (none in the course, but used in T4/T5 practice);
  sums of powers (2ⁿ+2ⁿ=2ⁿ⁺¹); numbers between 0 and 1 (powers, roots, reciprocals); a+b & ab → a²+b²; comparing powers
  (2³⁰ vs 3²⁰); conjugates; combining inequalities; |x−a| as distance; √n prime test; sums of consecutive numbers;
  carries and AA=11A; number-line picture questions; "which numbers to plug in" list; fractional exponents before use.
- **Word problems:** percent multipliers and percentage points; 2×2 table and three-group overlap; averages "balance"
  method; worker joins/leaves; rate % → time; train length, current, circular track both ways, distance–speed–time
  table; counting (glue together, round table, identical letters, zero-digit cases, at least one = all − none);
  tree diagrams, unknown number of tokens; pigeonhole; three-part ratios, inverse proportion; geometric probability
  (missing from the whole course).
- **Geometry:** bent-line angle rule, perpendicular-to-same-line; exterior angle (called "optional", used 8×), acute/
  obtuse test, area two ways, max area ½ab; trapezoid midsegment and diagonal areas; chord bisected by the
  perpendicular, inscribed-circle tangents, common tangent; exterior angles and diagonal count of polygons, polygons
  meeting at a point; water level and units (1 L = 1000 cm³), painted cubes; map scale, parallel-line proportion;
  quadrants, midpoint, reflection, line equations; "two sides and a changing angle".
- **Across the course:** "must be / could be / cannot be" — taught in T20 but needed from T11 on; teach it early.
