### Q 1 (q-120): What is the value of the expression: \({\left(a + b\right)}^{2} - {\left(a - b\right)}^{2} = ?\)
  (1) \(a - b\)
  (2) \({a}^{2} - {b}^{2}\)
  (3) \(0\)
  (4) \(4\,ab\)
  correct: 3
  solution: Expand both squares: (a² + 2ab + b²) − (a² − 2ab + b²) . The a² and b² terms cancel; the middle terms give 2ab − (−2ab) = 4ab .

### Q 2 (q-121): What is the value of the expression: \(\left(m - 2\right)\left(m + 2\right) - \left(n - 2\right)\left(n + 2\right) = ?\)

### Q None (q-100): \(8\,a + 5\,b - 3\,a - 2\,b = ?\)
  (1) \(3\,a + 5\,b\)
  (2) \(5\,a + 3\,b\)
  (3) \(5\,\left(a + b\right)\)
  (4) \(5\,a + 2\,b\)
  correct: 1
  solution: Group like terms: the a-terms give 8a − 3a = 5a , and the b-terms give 5b − 2b = 3b . So the expression simplifies to 5a + 3b .

### Q None (q-101): \(-\sqrt{5} - 7\,\sqrt{2} + 2\,\sqrt{2} + 3\,\sqrt{5} = ?\)
  (1) \(2\,\sqrt{5} - 7\,\sqrt{2}\)
  (2) \(2\,\sqrt{5} + 5\,\sqrt{2}\)
  (3) \(4\,\sqrt{5} - 5\,\sqrt{2}\)
  (4) \(2\,\sqrt{5} - 5\,\sqrt{2}\)
  correct: 3
  solution: Treat √5 and √2 exactly like two different letters. The √5 -terms: −√5 + 3√5 = 2√5 . The √2 -terms: −7√2 + 2√2 = −5√2 . Together: 2√5 − 5√2 .

### Q None (q-102): \(\left(-4x\right) \cdot  6\,z \cdot  2\,y = ?\)
  (1) \(-xyz\)
  (2) \(-12xyz\)
  (3) \(-24xyz\)
  (4) \(-48xyz\)
  correct: 3
  solution: Multiply the numbers together and the letters together: (−4) · 6 · 2 = −48 , and x · z · y = xyz . Result: −48xyz .

### Q None (q-103): \(5 \cdot  \left(-a\right) \cdot  {a}^{3} \cdot  \left(-2a\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(-10{a}^{5}\)
  (2) \(10\,{a}^{5}\)
  (3) \(10\,{a}^{4}\)
  (4) \(-10{a}^{4}\)
  correct: 1
  solution: Coefficients: 5 · (−1) · (−2) = 10 — two negatives cancel. Variables: a · a³ · a = a¹⁺³⁺¹ = a⁵ . Result: 10a⁵ .

### Q None (q-104): \(3\,x \cdot  2\,{x}^{2}y \cdot  \left(-4{xy}^{2}\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(-9{x}^{4}{y}^{3}\)
  (2) \(-24{x}^{4}{y}^{3}\)
  (3) \(-24{x}^{3}{y}^{3}\)
  (4) \(-24{x}^{3}{y}^{4}\)
  correct: 1
  solution: Coefficients: 3 · 2 · (−4) = −24 . x-exponents: x · x² · x = x⁴ . y-exponents: y · y² = y³ . Result: −24x⁴y³ .

### Q None (q-105): \(-4\left(3\,x - 5\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(-12x + 20\)
  (2) \(12\,x - 20\)
  (3) \(-12x - 20\)
  (4) \(12\,x + 20\)
  correct: 0
  solution: Distribute −4 over both terms: −4 · 3x = −12x and −4 · (−5) = +20 . Result: −12x + 20 .

### Q None (q-106): \(\left(x - 1\right)\left({x}^{3} + {x}^{2} + x + 1\right) = ?\)
  (1) \({x}^{4} + 1\)
  (2) \({x}^{4}\)
  (3) \({x}^{4} - 1\)
  (4) \(1\)
  correct: 2
  solution: Expand term by term: x·(x³+x²+x+1) = x⁴+x³+x²+x , and −1·(x³+x²+x+1) = −x³−x²−x−1 . Adding, every middle term cancels in pairs, leaving x⁴ − 1 .

### Q None (q-107): \(\left(p - q\right) - \left(q - p\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(0\)
  (2) \(2\,p - 2\,q\)
  (3) \(-2q\)
  (4) \(2\,p\)
  correct: 1
  solution: Open the second bracket with sign flips: (p − q) − (q − p) = p − q − q + p = 2p − 2q .

### Q None (q-108): \(\left(3\,a - b\right) - \left(-b + 3\,a\right) = ?\)

  (2) \(6\,a - 2\,b\)
  (3) \(-2b\)
  (4) \(0\)
  correct: 3

### Q None (q-109): \(\left(-q + p\right) - \left(-p - q\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(2\,p\)
  (2) \(-2q\)
  (3) \(2\,p - 2\,q\)
  (4) \(0\)
  correct: 0
  solution: Open the second bracket with sign flips: −q + p + p + q = 2p . The q's cancel; the p's double.

### Q None (q-110): \(\left(m - n\right) - \left(-n - \left(-m\right)\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(0\)
  (2) \(2\,m - 2\,n\)
  (3) \(-2n\)

### Q None (q-111): \({\left(3\,x + 4\right)}^{2} = ?\)
  (1) \(9\,{x}^{2} + 16\)
  (2) \(9\,{x}^{2} + 16 + 12\,x\)
  (3) \(9\,{x}^{2} + 16 + 24\,x\)
  (4) \(9\,{x}^{2} + 16 + 48\,x\)
  correct: 2
  solution: Use (A + B)² = A² + 2AB + B² with A = 3x , B = 4 : (3x)² = 9x² , 2 · 3x · 4 = 24x , 4² = 16 . So (3x + 4)² = 9x² + 24x + 16 .

### Q None (q-112): \(3\,{\left(x + 3\right)}^{2} = ?\)
  (1) \(3\,{x}^{2} + 27\)

### Q None (q-113): \({x}^{2} + 14\,x + 49 = ?\)  (factor)
  (1) \({\left(x + 4\right)}^{2}\)

### Q None (q-114): \({\left(2\,x - 3\right)}^{2} = ?\)

### Q None (q-115): \({\left(a - b\right)}^{2} - {\left(a + b\right)}^{2} = ?\)

### Q None (q-116): \(4\,{x}^{2} + 49 - 28\,x = ?\)  (factor)
  (1) \(2\,{\left(x - 7\right)}^{2}\)

### Q None (q-117): \(\left(x - 8\right)\left(x + 8\right) = ?\)

### Q None (q-119): \(16 - 9\,{x}^{2} = ?\)  (factor)

### Q None (alg-extra-unit-t4-1-1): Simplify \(3x+5y-2x-3y\).
  (1) \(1x+8y\)
  (2) \(3xy\)
  (3) \(1x+2y\)
  (4) \(5x+8y\)
