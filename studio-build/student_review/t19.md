### Q 1 (q-541): The operation ◆ is defined for every number a as: \(\blacklozenge \left(a\right) = {a}^{2} - 2\,a\). \(\blacklozenge \left(5\right) = ?\)
  (1) \(0\)
  (2) \(25\)
  (3) \(15\)
  (4) \(10\)
  correct: 2
  solution: Substitute a = 5 into the definition: ◆(5) = 5² − 2·5 = 25 − 10 = 15 .
